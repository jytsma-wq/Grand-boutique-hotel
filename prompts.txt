Phase 5: Image Migration to Sanity
Migrate external Unsplash images to Sanity media library. Currently, the project uses Unsplash URLs directly in the code, which has several disadvantages: no optimization control, reliance on external service, and missed SEO opportunities with alt text management. Migrating to Sanity provides better control over image optimization and ensures images are served through the Sanity CDN with proper transformations.
Benefits
•	Full control over image optimization and transformations
•	Consistent CDN delivery through Sanity
•	Better SEO with managed alt text in CMS
•	No dependency on external image services
•	Automatic WebP/AVIF conversion via Sanity
